Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains how the Smart Deals Uganda website and installable web app handle information. Smart Deals Uganda provides selected product listings and links to Jumia. It is not an official Jumia website.

Information handled by this app

You do not need an account to browse the catalogue. Cart items are stored in this browser's local storage. If you choose Flutterwave checkout, your name, email address, mobile number, selected mobile money network, and cart information are sent to this site's Netlify server function to create a charge and to Flutterwave to process and verify it. Flutterwave handles payment authorization under its own privacy terms. The site stores a pending transaction reference in this browser so it can check payment status after you return. Do not use payment checkout unless the amount and merchant details shown by Flutterwave are correct. If you choose WhatsApp checkout, a prefilled message containing product names, quantities, and any listed prices opens in WhatsApp; it is sent only if you choose to send it. The service worker caches app files on your device so the site can load reliably.

When you visit the site, Netlify may process standard hosting and security information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs. Product images are requested from Jumia's image host when displayed.

External links and services

Jumia product and category buttons use our JForce affiliate link. When you follow a link, Jumia and its affiliate technology providers may process referral identifiers, cookies, device or network information, and purchase activity to attribute eligible orders. Smart Deals Uganda does not receive your Jumia account credentials, payment details, or order history through this app. Commission reporting is available through the JForce account.

The on-site chat assistant does not save chat messages. When you submit a message, the site opens a prefilled WhatsApp message to Smart Deals Uganda; WhatsApp sends it only after you tap Send there. WhatsApp, phone, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok links open third-party services only when you choose them. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

If Google Analytics is enabled, Google may receive page and interaction events, device/browser information, and online identifiers to report site usage. If Tawk.to chat is enabled, Tawk.to may process chat messages and technical information when the chat widget loads or is used. These providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

Sharing and retention

Smart Deals Uganda does not sell personal information or operate a customer account database. Browser-stored catalogue data remains on that device until its site data is cleared. Hosting logs, affiliate attribution, and information handled by external services are governed by those providers' retention practices and policies.

Children

This service is a general shopping catalogue and is not specifically directed to children. It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

This policy may be updated as the service changes. For privacy questions, call Smart Deals Uganda at +256 700 321 960.
